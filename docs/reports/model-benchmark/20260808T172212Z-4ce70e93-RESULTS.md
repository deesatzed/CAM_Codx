## Recommendation

  quality/cost balance, but two fixtures contained at least one unverified
  provenance reference. Do not promote yet.
- Provisional cost/speed leader: `z-ai/glm-5.2`. It was cheapest and fast, but
  produced only one finding per fixture and one fixture had invalid provenance.
- Quality/coverage candidate: `x-ai/grok-4.5`. It produced the most findings,
  but was much slower, more expensive, and truncated one response.
- Batch candidate: Gemini batch transport works, but the current prompt/output
  budget truncated all three jobs. It is not ready for mining selection.
- Do not use Kimi K3 or Qwen 3.8 Max for this CAM prompt as currently configured.

## Root cause requiring a validation round

The production prompt asks for up to 15 detailed findings while the production
output ceilings are 4,096 or 6,144 tokens. This creates pressure to truncate,
emit meta-reasoning, or compress provenance. A follow-up design should cap the
requested findings to the output budget and explicitly require exact file paths
and exact identifiers (or omission of uncertain symbols), then rerun Luna, GLM,
Grok, and Gemini on frozen fixtures. That behavior change was not applied
automatically.
